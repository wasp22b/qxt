Practical energy simulation

Inputs are hourly forecasts. A multi-period model chooses source-to-zone kWh, renewable-to-thermal charging, and heat inventory. It models supply/feeder/route limits and prohibits simultaneous charging and discharge with binary modes.

SOC[t+1] = SOC[t] + η_charge × charge[t]
                        − electric_discharge[t] / η_discharge
reserve ≤ SOC[t] ≤ storage_capacity
Σ_zone allocation[t, source, zone] + charge[t, source]
                                       ≤ available_source[t]
Σ_source allocation[t, source, zone] + unmet[t, zone]
                                       = demand[t, zone]

Backup is real-time emergency generation, never stored energy. Storage is explicitly heat-to-power, not heat directly satisfying electrical load. Unused dispatchable capacity is not waste. Unused renewable potential is reported separately; exports are recommendations only.

Lexicographic decisions

  1. Minimize critical-service minimum violations.
  2. Minimize household minimum violations.
  3. Minimize total unmet demand.
  4. Minimize required emergency generation.
  5. Maximize the sum of hourly minimum noncritical served fractions.
  6. Minimize energy cost + optional carbon price.

Earlier objective optima are retained to numerical tolerance. Cost minimization operates inside these safety/fairness rules, not instead of them. In physically impossible conditions, minimum violations are displayed explicitly. Fairness uses served/demand ratios; Jain's index is descriptive, not the objective.

Quantum formulation & honest claims

g_s = Σ_b weight_sb x_sb,  x_sb ∈ {0,1}
0 ≤ g_s ≤ bounded_capacity_s   (built into encoding)
Σ_s g_s = target_blocks
E(x) = normalized_cost(x) + 2 (Σ_s g_s − target_blocks)²
x_i = (1 − Z_i) / 2
|ψ(γ,β)⟩ = ∏_layers [exp(−i β Σ X) exp(−i γ H_cost)] |+⟩^n

Feasible objective values lie in [0,1]; an invalid integer equality residual incurs at least 2, so ground states match the discrete optimum. Every bitstring is checked against the original constraints. P(optimal) for a uniform circuit counts all optimal encodings, including duplicates. CVaR averages the lowest-energy probability mass with fractional boundary handling.

QAOA has no established speedup here. An ideal simulator is classical computation of quantum dynamics. Entangling gates can create entanglement; they do not guarantee entanglement at every angle. Higher depth does not prove quantum advantage. The baseline p=0 and exact enumeration reveal probability amplification on this specific instance.

Known boundaries

No AC power flow, voltage/frequency control, ramping, start-up costs, stochastic uncertainty, equipment degradation, hardware noise, live utility data, or actuators. Demand profiles are scenario generation, not a trained forecast. Re-optimize remaining hours with the latest measured SOC for an operational rolling-horizon loop. This is a decision-support prototype, not a safety-certified grid controller.
